# Credits

Heroes of Twilight would not exist without the work that came before it.

This project is built on **Crests of Courage by remiafterdark**. I did
not create the original Twilight Princess multiplayer foundation, and I
do not want the work of the people who made that possible to get lost
just because this project has a different name.

## Heroes of Twilight

**ThyHeroOfTime**

Heroes of Twilight is my expanded version of the multiplayer experience.
I started working on it mostly because I wanted to play Twilight
Princess with my kids.

My work on the project focuses on expanding and reorganizing the
multiplayer experience, improving the player-facing interface, and
building out the separate Twilight Connected and Hyrule Online
experiences while keeping the original multiplayer foundation intact.

## Crests of Courage

**remiafterdark**

Crests of Courage is the original multiplayer project that Heroes of
Twilight is based on.

The core multiplayer foundation, including the systems Heroes of
Twilight was built from and continues to rely on, comes from Crests of
Courage.

Original project:

https://github.com/remiafterdark/crests-of-courage

Heroes of Twilight is not intended to replace Crests of Courage or claim
its original work as my own.
